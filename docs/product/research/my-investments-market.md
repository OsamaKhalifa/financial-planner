# Market research — "My Investments" (manual list, ROI, real return)

**Status:** v0 · 2026-10-16 · PM hat
**Question:** how do good apps handle a hand-entered investment list, return on investment (ROI), and inflation-adjusted ("real") return — and what should we copy or skip?
**Method:** vendor help pages, app store listings, community posts (web search, 2026-10-16). No app was installed or tested. "Vendor-stated" = the vendor's own claim, not checked by us. "Not verified" = we found no source.

## Takeaways

1. **Nobody we checked shows return after zakat and after inflation together.** The general apps do return well but ignore zakat; the Islamic apps do zakat but measure no return. This is our gap — and the reason the feature is worth building. (Absence is "not found", not proven.)
2. **Return must come from cash flows, not from "value ÷ cost".** Sharesight and Kubera both base return on money in / money out. Without this, a monthly saver (Yasmin) sees her new deposits counted as profit.
3. **Modified Dietz is the right v1 method.** Sharesight uses a variant for private investors; it needs no iteration (IRR can fail) and no valuation at every cash flow. Its known limit (drifts from IRR over long periods with big flows) is acceptable and can be stated.
4. **Do not annualise periods under one year** (Sharesight rule). Annualising 3% in one month to 42% a year is misleading.
5. **Skip time-weighted return.** Getquin's own help shows it can say "positive" while the user lost money. Our question is "did *my* wealth keep its value", which is money-weighted.
6. **Keep every value update as history, and show when a value is old** (Kubera). With manual entry, staleness is the biggest quality risk; hiding it makes month views lie.
7. **Zakatable base can be a share of value** (Wahed uses proxies). So the item needs a zakat share, not only yes/no. The numbers must come from our Islamic consultant.
8. *(Superseded 2026-10-16 by owner decision Q2: v1 adds an optional, user-triggered online refresh of FX and gold/silver rates — see the story §6.1.)* **Manual entry is enough for v1.** No evidence forces a price API: Kubera's core use is manual, and Musaffa offers manual add. A price feed would also break "no data leaves the device". It stays an open decision (BRD §9 #1), not an assumption.
9. **Inflation is always a user-visible assumption** where it exists (Snowball shows and lets the user edit assumptions). We do the same: the user enters the rate; we never hide it.
